raw-radio-studio — macOS install notes (UNSIGNED build)
======================================================

This build is NOT code-signed or notarized (notarization is deferred, see
STUDIO_SPEC §8.5). macOS Gatekeeper will therefore refuse to open it on the
first launch. This is expected, not a sign of a corrupt download.

Install
-------
1. Open the .dmg and drag "raw-radio-studio.app" into your Applications folder.

First launch (Gatekeeper bypass)
--------------------------------
Option A — right-click to open (no Terminal):
  1. In Finder, open Applications.
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) "raw-radio-studio.app" and choose "Open".
  3. Click "Open" again in the dialog. macOS remembers this choice.

Option B — Terminal (remove the quarantine flag):
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/raw-radio-studio.app

Requirements
------------
  * macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer.
  * Microphone access is requested on first audio use.
