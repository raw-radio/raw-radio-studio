LV2 SDK (bundled with JUCE)

Source: third_party/JUCE (pinned tag 9.0.3),
        modules/juce_audio_processors_headless/format_types/LV2_SDK/
License (SPDX): ISC (permissive)

LV2 hosting is opt-in (JUCE_PLUGINHOST_LV2, enabled with
-DRAW_RADIO_STUDIO_ENABLE_LV2=ON). The LV2 SDK — lv2, lilv, serd, sord, and
sratom — is bundled inside JUCE's pinned tree; no separate download is
performed, and no external system LV2 libraries are required. Each component is
distributed under a permissive ISC-style license, reproduced verbatim below
(from each component's COPYING file).
